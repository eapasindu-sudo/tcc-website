About us

A home of learning for Thelijjawila

Thelijjawila Central College is a mixed national school for boys and girls in Thelijjawila, Matara, in the Southern Province. It stands on a hillock at Udukawa facing the paddy fields, close to the Weligama–Akuressa main road. More than 2,500 students in Grades 6 to 13 learn here in Sinhala and English, guided by 130 teachers.

The college was founded in 1946 as a Buddhist school under the education-for-villages scheme of Dr. C. W. W. Kannangara, the Father of Free Education, with the support of David Wanigasekara and Ven. Naranda Thero of Wajiraramaya, Bambalapitiya. It was declared a national school on 31 January 1996.
